The North American index

The North American Diesel Index (NADI) is the mean of the Canadian national average and the US national average — each country counts once, not consumption-weighted. This week the NADI is 214.1¢/L, with Canada Canada higher by 28.6¢/L.

Methodology: /methodology/nmdi/. The Canadian half is the NRCan weekly survey (ten provinces); the US half is the EIA national average above.
