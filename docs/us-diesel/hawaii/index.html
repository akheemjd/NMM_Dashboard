The four numbers add up. State excise $0.1600 plus other state fees $0.0250 gives a state total of $0.1850. Add the federal $0.2440 and the all-in figure is $0.4290 a gallon.

Hawaii is not an IFTA jurisdiction, so no IFTA rate exists to file for it. That is not a gap in this page. The statutory excise above is the figure that applies.

The four figures above are the whole picture for Hawaii. The statutory excise is what the state levies on a gallon of diesel sold there, other state fees are added on top, and the federal rate applies everywhere in the country.

Why there is no Hawaii diesel price here

The Energy Information Administration publishes a weekly on-highway diesel price on a district basis, not a state basis. There are eleven districts in the whole country and Hawaii is not one of them.

Hawaii sits in the West Coast (PADD 5) district. The figure at the top of this page is that district's weekly price. It is not a Hawaii price, and we are not going to label it as one.

Plenty of sites will show you a Hawaii diesel price. What they are showing you is the same district number they show for every neighbouring state, because the state-level number does not exist to show. If a site claims a per-state diesel price, ask it which survey it came from.
