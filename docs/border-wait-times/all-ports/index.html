How to read this

These are US-bound waits. The Canada-bound direction is a separate feed published by the Canada Border Services Agency, and it is on the crossing pages. A carrier crossing south checks here; a carrier coming home checks there.

Only 38 of 85 ports had published a commercial truck delay when this page was last built. A port with no published figure is shown as not reported, never as zero. Zero means the agency measured no wait. Not reported means the agency did not say, and those are not the same thing to a driver deciding whether to roll now or wait an hour.
