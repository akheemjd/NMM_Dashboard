How this is worked out

Plain arithmetic, no hidden assumptions.

Litres burneddistance ÷ 100 × consumptionL
Trip costlitres × price per litre$
Cost per kilometretrip cost ÷ distance$/km

The prices offered here are retail survey averages from Natural Resources Canada, inclusive of federal and provincial fuel taxes, carbon pricing, and sales taxes. They are not rack prices and not what a fleet on a fuel card pays, which is usually lower. If you know your card price, enter it.

Consumption is the field that changes the answer most, and it is the one we refuse to guess at. A loaded highway tractor and a light-load regional run can differ by a third or more. Pull the figure from your own records rather than accepting a number off a website.
