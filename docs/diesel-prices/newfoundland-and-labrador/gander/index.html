What drives Gander prices

Gander sits in central Newfoundland, on Highway 1 between the island's east and west ends. The town carries a long aviation history, tied to Gander International. That legacy still shapes the place. On the road network it is a midpoint, a stop on the island's main corridor rather than a terminus. A carrier passing through finds a town defined by its airport and its place in the middle of the island, not by any single industry that sets its fuel market apart.
