What drives Barrie prices

Barrie sits on the Highway 400 corridor north of Toronto, and warehousing and distribution are where its growth is. The city moves goods up and down the 400 series network, feeding the Toronto market from the north. That distribution role shapes the local diesel market. A corridor town built around storage and freight movement runs on that flow, not on any single industry. The warehousing boom along the 400 is the story here, and it is what the fuel market follows.
