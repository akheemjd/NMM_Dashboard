What drives Guelph prices

Guelph sits on the Highway 401 and Highway 6 corridors and runs on manufacturing and agri-food. The city's economy blends industrial output with food processing, and both move freight. Trucks haul manufactured goods and food products in and out of Guelph on a steady basis. That mix of industry and agri-food is what shapes fuel demand here. Its spot on the 401 ties it directly into the wider southern Ontario network, which keeps its market connected to the regional flow of goods.
