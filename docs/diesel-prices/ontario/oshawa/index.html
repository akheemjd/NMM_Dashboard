What drives Oshawa prices

Oshawa sits on the Highway 401 corridor and runs on auto assembly, with General Motors anchoring the city's industrial base. Auto assembly means a steady flow of trucks hauling parts in and finished vehicles out, which keeps the local freight network busy. That automotive base is what drives fuel demand here. Oshawa's spot on the 401 ties it into the main southern Ontario freight corridor, and the plant work keeps demand steady rather than seasonal. A city built around one big industry runs on that industry's rhythm.
