Across the line

Diesel crosses the border, but the number does not. The Canadian side is published per litre and the American side per gallon.

Central Atlantic · the US district next door$6.485/gal

The American figure is the EIA weekly retail survey — published as $6.485/gal this week — shown the way an American carrier reads it, with the Canadian-dollar equivalent beside it once the page loads. A gallon is not a litre, so these two lines measure the same fuel in different units and the cents will never match. What does compare is the market: a load crossing into Central Atlantic buys its fuel on the other side of a border.

What drives Hamilton prices

Hamilton is Canada's steel heart. ArcelorMittal Dofasco and Stelco anchor a heavy industrial base, and the city is a major Great Lakes port. Steel and shipping mean constant freight, with trucks moving raw material and finished metal around the clock. Hamilton sits at the junction of the QEW, Highway 403, and the LINC, so it ties into the regional highway network from every direction. That steel and port economy, more than anything, is what sets the character of the local fuel market.
