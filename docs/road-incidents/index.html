What a closure costs

A lane closure is not a delay, it is a decision. A single-lane night closure on a corridor carrying thousands of trucks a day moves the queue rather than removing it, and the cost lands as driver hours against a driving limit, not as a line on an invoice.

Read the type before the location. Closures are the ones that reroute you. Roadwork is scheduled and usually carries a date range, so it belongs in planning rather than in today's decision. This page separates the two and invents neither: both come straight from the agency's own feed, with the coordinates the agency published.

Coverage is honest about its edges. Eight states on the federal work zone standard, three California districts, Ontario and British Columbia serve open feeds. Other states and provinces publish behind keys we do not hold, and Caltrans serves only some of its districts. Where a source is missing it is missing from the page, not estimated into it.
