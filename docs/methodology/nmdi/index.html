Northern Mile Diesel Index (NMDI)

v1.0 · methodology

The Northern Mile Diesel Index (NMDI) is our daily measurement of retail diesel prices by province. It is a named proprietary series with a published methodology.

What it measures

Average pump price of diesel paid by truck operators in each Canadian province. Reflects what a carrier pays at the pump, not the wholesale rack price.

Collection

Compiled from provincial retail diesel surveys updated weekly. One representative average per province from major urban markets. Ten provinces. National figure is the arithmetic mean.

Cadence

Updated every 30 minutes. Underlying survey refreshes weekly. Between updates, the NMDI holds its last reading — it does not interpolate.

Revisions

NMDI values are never silently changed. Corrections are versioned and published in the corrections log.

Version

NMDI v1.0 — established August 2026.
