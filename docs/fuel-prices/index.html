Common questions

Why is diesel different across provinces?
Provincial fuel taxes drive most of the gap. BC adds carbon tax and transit levies; Alberta has lower levies and no provincial sales tax. The AB–QC spread is 49.6c/L.
How often do prices update?
The NRCan survey publishes weekly. This page rebuilds every 30 minutes from the most recent survey, so the diesel figure holds steady between prints. See the NMDI methodology.
Do these prices include carbon tax?
Yes — all federal and provincial taxes are included in the pump price shown.
