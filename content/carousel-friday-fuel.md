# Friday Carousel Template — Fuel Prices

Build a 5-slide LinkedIn carousel PDF for Northern Mile Media. Export as 1080×1080 px square slides, PDF format. Dark theme.

## Design System
Background: #0B0D11 (deep blue-black)
Primary text: #F2F4F7 (near-white)
Secondary text: #A8B2BE (gray)
Accent: #1E9E66 (green)
Yellow: #F5C518
Red: #E5484D
Font: system sans-serif (no web fonts needed)

## Brand
Northern Mile Media — Canadian trucking dashboard and newsletter. Voice: dispatcher-analyst. Data-first, no fluff.

## Data (pull live from dashboard before building)
Fetch: https://dashboard.northernmilemedia.com/data/fuel.json
→ diesel national avg, all province prices, BC-AB spread

## Slide Content

### Slide 1 — Cover
Logo: "NORTHERN MILE" top left, green. Slide number: "1/5" top right, gray.
Headline: "What diesel costs this week" (or hook with the biggest gap/move)
Subtitle: "Every province. Every pump. One place."
Footer: Today's date

### Slide 2 — The National Picture
Big number centered: [national avg] in green, with "c/L" in gray
Subtitle: "Canadian diesel national average"
Below: "Unchanged for X weeks" or "Down/Up X.Xc this week"
Footer: "Updated every 30 minutes from public surveys"

### Slide 3 — The Spread (always include BC-AB)
Headline: "The spread that costs real money"
Visual: BC [178.5] vs AB [158.9] — large contrast
Below: "19.6 cents. That's $78 on a 400-litre fill."
Subtitle: "Fill in Alberta. Every time."
Footer: "Every province, every pump — live at dashboard.northernmilemedia.com"

### Slide 4 — Cost Per Lane
Headline: "What a lane actually costs"
Example: "Calgary → Vancouver: 970 km at 38 L/100km = $XXX in fuel"
Or show 3 sample lanes with fuel costs
Footer: "Calculate your own at the fuel cost calculator"

### Slide 5 — CTA
Headline: "We track diesel across all 10 provinces. Check before you leave the yard."
URL in green: "northernmilemedia.com"
Subtitle: "Get the Wednesday Snapshot"
Footer: "dashboard.northernmilemedia.com — free, no signup, updated every 30 min"

## Rules
No emojis, no AI vocabulary, no fluff. Clean typography, strong hierarchy. Dark theme. 5 pages, 1080×1080 each.
