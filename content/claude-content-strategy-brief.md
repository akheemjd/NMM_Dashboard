# Northern Mile Media — Content Strategy Brief

## Background

Northern Mile Media runs a free live data dashboard for Canadian trucking at dashboard.northernmilemedia.com. The dashboard has 9 pages of live data: diesel prices by province, border wait times, USD/CAD exchange rate, road incidents, cargo theft, market pulse, industry news, fuel cost calculator, and a cockpit home page.

We have 3 content channels that are currently unbuilt:
1. Blog (Ghost CMS at northernmilemedia.com)
2. Newsletter (weekly email)
3. LinkedIn (business page)

## What We Need From You

Design a complete content strategy that answers:

### 1. Role Definition
What is the specific role of each channel? Blog vs newsletter vs LinkedIn. Why would someone read one but not the others? What does each channel own that the others don't?

### 2. Publishing Cadence  
How often does each channel publish? Specific days and times. A calendar for one month.

### 3. Content Strategy  
What kind of content goes where? Examples of headlines/topics for each channel. What voice does each channel use? Are they the same voice or different?

### 4. Source to Channel Pipeline
The dashboard is the source of truth. How does data flow from dashboard → blog → newsletter → LinkedIn? Is it a funnel? Are they independent? Does the blog feed the newsletter or vice versa?

### 5. Audience Growth  
How does each channel grow? What SEO strategy for the blog? What's the newsletter signup mechanism? What drives LinkedIn followers?

### 6. Monetization Path
How does this make money? Timeline. When does newsletter sponsorship kick in? What role does the dashboard play? Does the blog make money directly or just funnel?

### 7. Automation Logic
Each channel needs to be automated. Design the logic for automated content generation. What triggers a post? What data feeds into each channel? What does the automation pipeline look like?

## Constraints

- All data comes from the dashboard. No external paid sources.
- Blog + newsletter + LinkedIn are separate channels. No copy-paste cross-posting. Each earns its own audience.
- Dashboard is free forever. Monetization comes from newsletter sponsorship.
- Voice: dispatcher-analyst. "We." Data-first. No AI vocabulary. No emojis. No filler.
- Target audience: Canadian fleet owners, dispatchers, owner-operators.

## What You Deliver

A markdown document with the full strategy. Be specific. No vague recommendations. Include:
- A content calendar template
- A sample blog post outline
- A sample newsletter structure
- A sample LinkedIn post
- An automation workflow diagram (text-based, not an image)
- The monetization timeline
