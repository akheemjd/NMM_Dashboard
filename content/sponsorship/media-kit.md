# Northern Mile Media — Sponsorship Media Kit

## The one-liner

**Northern Mile Media is the source of truth for Canadian trucking.** A free, live
data dashboard plus a weekly email that tells dispatchers and fleet operators what
changed and what to do about it — before their competition does.

---

## The product: The Wednesday Fuel & Freight Snapshot

A weekly email, every Wednesday at 7:00 AM ET. 200–300 words, a 90-second read.
Fuel prices, exchange rate, border waits, road incidents — the numbers that move a
carrier's margin, with the "so what" attached.

Not a news digest. Competitive intelligence. **Know before they do.**

---

## Audience

Canadian trucking decision-makers — the people who check fuel prices, FX, and
border waits every morning:

- Fleet operators and dispatchers
- Owner-operators
- Freight brokers and 3PLs
- Shippers moving freight across the border

They buy fuel cards, insurance, ELDs, factoring, customs brokerage, and equipment
finance — the exact categories our sponsors sell. They don't read marketing email.
They read numbers. You reach them where they already are.

## Why sponsor the Snapshot

- **Trust by association.** Our brand is built on unbiased, primary-source data
  (Natural Resources Canada, Bank of Canada, CBSA). Readers trust us because we show
  our work. Your brand sits inside that trust.
- **A hard-to-reach audience.** Dispatchers and owner-operators are notoriously
  hard to reach with display or social ads. They open a data email.
- **Contextual placement.** Your category, placed against the data it relates to.
- **No competition for attention.** One sponsor per placement. Exclusivity.
