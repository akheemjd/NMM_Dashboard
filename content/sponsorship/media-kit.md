# Northern Mile Media — Sponsorship

---

## What Northern Mile is

A free, live data dashboard for Canadian trucking, and one email a week about it.

Every figure comes from a primary source, is dated, and carries a copy-paste
citation: the Natural Resources Canada weekly retail survey, the Canada Border
Services Agency, the Bank of Canada and the US Energy Information
Administration. If we get something wrong we correct it and say what changed.

The dashboard is 108 pages: 69 city diesel pages, all ten provinces, five US
PADD regions, and nine commercial border crossings. It is free and stays free.

---

## The product: The Northern Mile Brief

One email, Wednesday mornings at 06:00 ET. Roughly 240 words. Fuel by province,
border wait times, USD/CAD, and the one number worth acting on before the next
set of fills.

Not a news digest. The numbers that move a carrier's margin, with the source
attached.
