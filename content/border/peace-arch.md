Peace Arch is the passenger crossing at Blaine, Washington, and one of the two CBP ports that share the Blaine complex alongside the Pacific Highway commercial route. The two are minutes apart and serve the same corridor into British Columbia, but they are built for different traffic.

This one handles cars. CBP's commercial reading at Peace Arch is frequently absent, and this site reports that as not reported rather than as a clear commercial lane. A driver taking that absence for an opening would be making exactly the mistake the distinction exists to prevent.

The arch itself marks the boundary between the two countries at the western end of the border and is the crossing most travellers picture. For freight the answer is the Pacific Highway port next door, which is the dedicated commercial route for the Vancouver-Seattle corridor. Knowing which of the two is which saves a driver a wasted stop.
