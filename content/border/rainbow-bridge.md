Rainbow Bridge is the downtown Niagara Falls crossing, and it is the one of the three Niagara bridges that carries essentially no commercial traffic. The Peace Bridge and the Lewiston-Queenston bridge either side of it handle the freight; Rainbow is the tourist crossing and the view.

CBP usually has no commercial reading here, and this site reports that as not reported rather than as a zero. A port that sees no trucks for most of the day is not a clear commercial lane, and showing it as one would be actively misleading to a driver deciding where to cross.

The useful thing to know about it is its position. Rainbow sits between the two freight crossings on the Niagara river, so a carrier working that corridor should treat the three as a set. The commercial answer is almost always the Peace Bridge or Lewiston, and Rainbow is the one to rule out first.
