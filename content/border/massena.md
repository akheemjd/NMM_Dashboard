Massena is a St Lawrence River crossing between northern New York and Cornwall, Ontario, and it serves the corridor that runs between the North Country and the Montreal area. It sits well east of the Thousand Islands crossing and well west of Champlain, which means for a stretch of the river it is the only practical option.

That position defines its traffic. This is not a corridor port carrying freeway volumes between major cities; it is a regional crossing serving industry, agriculture and local freight on both sides of the river. The Seaway and the surrounding towns generate steady commercial traffic rather than heavy through-freight.

Facilities are modest on both sides, and the approaches are local roads rather than an interstate. A carrier using this crossing is usually doing so deliberately, because the route suits the load, rather than arriving by default. Fuel exists in Massena and in Cornwall.
