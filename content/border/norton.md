Norton is a small Vermont-Quebec crossing on the road between Island Pond, Vermont and Coaticook, Quebec. It sits between the two freeway crossings of Derby Line and Highgate Springs, serving the rural country in the middle that neither of those routes reaches.

That is what defines it. This is not a corridor port and it does not carry through-freight between cities; it serves the lumber, agricultural and local traffic of a thinly populated region on both sides of the line. CBP readings here are steady but low, and a carrier should not expect volatility.

Facilities are minimal. Norton is a small settlement and the Canadian side is rural for a distance in either direction, so a driver should be fuelled and prepared before arriving rather than planning on services at the crossing. When the figure is quiet it is genuinely quiet, but there is nothing to divert to nearby.
