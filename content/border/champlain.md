Champlain is the main crossing on the New York-Quebec line, where Interstate 87 meets Quebec Autoroute 15. It is the busiest commercial port in the region and the direct route between Montreal and New York City, which makes it one of the more significant crossings on the eastern half of the border.

The corridor is the reason. Autoroute 15 runs north from the border to Montreal and Interstate 87 runs south to Albany and on to the metropolitan area, so the crossing sits at the midpoint of a continuous freeway between two major markets. A carrier running Montreal to New York does not consider an alternative; this is the route.

For that reason the port is built for volume, with multiple commercial lanes and the facilities of a major crossing. It also means that when it backs up, the queue is on a freeway rather than a town street, and the nearest meaningful alternative is a considerable distance west or east. Plan around the number rather than hoping.
