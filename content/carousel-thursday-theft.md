# Thursday Carousel Template — Cargo Theft

Build a 5-slide LinkedIn carousel PDF for Northern Mile Media. Export as 1080×1080 px square slides, PDF format. Dark theme.

## Design System
Background: #0B0D11 (deep blue-black)
Primary text: #F2F4F7 (near-white)
Secondary text: #A8B2BE (gray)
Accent: #1E9E66 (green)
Red: #E5484D
Yellow: #F5C518
Font: system sans-serif (no web fonts needed)

## Brand
Northern Mile Media — Canadian trucking dashboard and newsletter. Voice: dispatcher-analyst. Data-first, no fluff.

## Data (pull live from dashboard before building)
Fetch: https://dashboard.northernmilemedia.com/data/theft.json
→ incidents[0]: title, location, value, method, prevention

## Slide Content

### Slide 1 — Cover
Logo: "NORTHERN MILE" top left, green. Slide number: "1/5" top right, gray.
Headline: "[Location] this week" (e.g. "Brampton this week")
Subtitle: "[Value] in cargo. [Method]."
Footer: Today's date

### Slide 2 — The Incident
Headline: "[Title]" — big, centered
Below: Location · Value in large red text
Subtitle: What happened and when
Footer: "Canadian cargo theft — tracked every week"

### Slide 3 — How They Did It
Headline: "How it happened"
Description of the method (yard break-in, fictitious pickup, trailer stolen while driver inside)
One sentence on what made this possible
Footer: "Source: Northern Mile dashboard — live theft tracking"

### Slide 4 — Prevention
Headline: "What stops this"
One specific prevention tip directly relevant to this incident
Example: "High-security padlocks and motion lighting would have stopped this." or "Verify carrier credentials before releasing a load."
Footer: One prevention tip saves more than insurance ever pays out.

### Slide 5 — CTA
Headline: "We track cargo theft across Canada every week. Know what's happening on your lanes."
URL in green: "northernmilemedia.com"
Subtitle: "Get the Wednesday Snapshot"
Footer: "dashboard.northernmilemedia.com — free Canadian trucking data"

## Rules
No emojis, no AI vocabulary, no fluff. Clean typography, strong hierarchy. Dark theme. 5 pages, 1080×1080 each.
