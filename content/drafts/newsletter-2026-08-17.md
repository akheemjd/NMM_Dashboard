THE WEDNESDAY SNAPSHOT
August 17, 2026

Diesel jumped 5.7 cents in seven days. That is material, not noise.

## Diesel up 5.7 cents

National average hit 227.9 cents a litre. Up 5.7 cents in a week and 13.6 cents over thirty days. Newfoundland led the climb, up 14.6 on the week. At 35 litres per 100 km, that is $798 in fuel for every thousand kilometres.

## Loonie keeps firming

USD/CAD sits at 1.3875, down 0.45% on the day. A stronger loonie means a thousand US dollars now converts to C$1,387.50. Notable, not a rout.

## 44 cent spread

Alberta 205.6. Quebec 249.6. A 44.0 cent gap from the cheapest pump to the dearest. BC runs 30.1 cents above Alberta. Fill in Alberta. Every time.

## One thing to do

Top up before September 8. The federal excise, 4 cents a litre, returns that day.
