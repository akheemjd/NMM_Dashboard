# Northern Mile Briefing
## Friday 7 November 2026. Three documents, three costs.

### 1. Diesel printed at 258.1 a litre, and the average is the least useful number in it

The weekly survey printed Tuesday 3 November. National average 258.1 cents per litre.

Quebec is the dearest at 288.9. Northwest Territories is the cheapest at 226.3. The spread between them is 62.6 cents a litre.

A lane budget built on the national average is wrong in every market that lane touches. The truck does not buy the national average, it buys Quebec or it buys the territories, and those two are 62.6 cents apart on the same product. Book fuel at the price where the wheels actually stop.

[Provincial diesel prices](https://dashboard.northernmilemedia.com/diesel-prices/)

### 2. IFTA is still quoting the third quarter, and one jurisdiction quotes nothing

The quarterly matrix for 3Q2026 lists 58 jurisdictions. Ten are Canadian, 48 are American. The fourth-quarter matrix has not published, so any rate you reprice today is a third-quarter rate.

On the diesel side the American range runs from California at 0.979 a gallon to Oklahoma at 0.19. The Canadian range runs from Quebec at 0.202 a litre to Ontario at 0.09. One jurisdiction in the 58 carries no rate at all, which a careless import reads as zero.

The tax you file is not the tax you paid, and it is not one number. It is a rate per jurisdiction for every jurisdiction the truck ran through, and the matrix is the only place those rates are stated.

[Fuel tax rates by jurisdiction](https://dashboard.northernmilemedia.com/fuel-tax-rates/)

### 3. Two border boards gave two numbers for the same bridge

CBP's commercial board had 25 of its 85 ports reporting at fetch time. The deepest delay on it was 5 minutes, shared by Lynden in Washington, Commercial in California and San Luis II in Arizona.

On the same evening the Canadian crossings showed Ambassador at 15 minutes, Peace Bridge at 13, Queenston-Lewiston at 7 and Pacific Highway at 5.

Peace Bridge reads 13 minutes on one board and 4 on the other, and neither is wrong. One is the queue into Canada, the other is the queue into the United States. A quote that names a wait without naming the direction has named nothing.

The CBP board covers only the ports that filed. A blank there is not a clear lane, it is a port that did not report, and 25 of 85 reported.

[CBP port delays, every port](https://dashboard.northernmilemedia.com/border-wait-times/all-ports/)

### The archive, which is the part you cannot get elsewhere

55 crossings carry archived readings. Queenston Lewiston averages 9 minutes, sits at 27 on its 90th percentile, and has a worst reading of 33. Lacolle Champlain averages 2 minutes and carries a worst of 110. Pacific Blaine averages 10 with a worst of 70.

An average of 1 minute and a worst of 20 describe the same crossing, and only one of those two numbers tells a driver whether his reload makes it. Plan against the worst reading for the hour you are actually driving.
