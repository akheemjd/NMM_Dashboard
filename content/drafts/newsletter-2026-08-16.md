## Diesel up 5.7 cents

Up 5.7 cents in seven days. Up 28.7 cents in thirty days. Newfoundland led it, up 14.6 on the week. At 35 litres per 100 km, that is $798 in fuel per thousand kilometres.

Alberta 205.6. Quebec 249.6. A 44.0 cent spread from the cheapest pump to the dearest. BC sits 30.1 cents above Alberta. Fill in Alberta. Every time.

The only heavy crossing this week. Pacific Highway 5 minutes, Queenston-Lewiston 1. Everything else flowing.
