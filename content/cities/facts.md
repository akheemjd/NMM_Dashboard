# NMM City Facts — verified grounding for city-page prose

The writer may state ONLY what appears in this file or in the data brief.
Never invent a refinery, highway number, port, or industry. If a fact is not
here, do not assert it. Big markets (flagged) carry more depth; small markets
are 2–3 facts.

## Alberta

### Calgary — big market
- Highway hub: Hwy 1 (Trans-Canada) and Hwy 2 (QEII) meet here.
- Oil-patch service centre; corporate energy head offices.
- Usually the cheapest large fill in Alberta, close to the Edmonton-area refineries.

### Edmonton — big market
- Alberta's refining hub (Strathcona County refineries).
- Northern terminus of Hwy 2; gateway to the northern oil sands.
- Provincial capital and northern logistics anchor.

### Grande Prairie
- Northern Alberta oil-and-gas service hub on the Hwy 43 corridor.

### Lethbridge
- Southern Alberta agriculture and food-processing centre.
- Hwy 3/4 corridor; closest major market to the Coutts (Sweetgrass) border crossing.

### Lloydminster
- Straddles the Alberta–Saskatchewan border.
- Heavy-oil service town on the Hwy 16 Yellowhead corridor.

### Red Deer
- Midpoint of the Calgary–Edmonton run on Hwy 2.
- Oil-patch service centre for central Alberta.

## British Columbia

### Vancouver — big market
- Canada's largest port; Pacific gateway for container and bulk freight.
- Metro Vancouver transit tax adds to the pump price.
- No major local refinery; fuel arrives by the Trans Mountain pipeline from Alberta.
- Western terminus of Hwy 1 (Trans-Canada).

### Victoria
- Island market; fuel trucked or barged from the mainland, so it runs above Vancouver.

### Abbotsford
- Fraser Valley agriculture and trucking centre.
- On Hwy 1 near the Sumas (Huntingdon) US crossing; cheaper than Metro Vancouver.

### Kamloops
- Interior BC trucking junction: Hwy 1, Hwy 5 (Coquihalla), and Hwy 16 meet here.
- Distribution and transshipment hub for the southern interior.

### Kelowna
- Okanagan Valley service and distribution centre on the Hwy 97 corridor.

### Prince George
- Northern BC forestry and transport hub; Hwy 16 and Hwy 97 junction, "gateway to the north."

### Fort St. John
- Natural-gas service hub on the Hwy 97 (Alaska Highway) corridor; serves the Montney play.

## Manitoba

### Winnipeg — big market
- Geographic centre of North America; central distribution hub.
- Hwy 1 and Hwy 75 (to US I-29) corridor; major rail intermodal yard.

### Brandon
- Western Manitoba agriculture hub on the Trans-Canada (Hwy 1); rail yard.

## New Brunswick

### Saint John — big market
- Home to the Irving Oil refinery, the largest in Canada.
- Ice-free Atlantic port; Hwy 1 terminus.

### Moncton
- Eastern New Brunswick hub; Hwy 2/15 junction.
- Atlantic Canada's main distribution and logistics centre (rail + airport).

### Fredericton
- Provincial capital on the Hwy 2 (Trans-Canada) corridor; government and tech.

### Edmundston
- Northwestern New Brunswick on Hwy 2 at the Quebec border; pulp and paper.

### Bathurst
- Northern New Brunswick; mining and forestry heritage; Hwy 11 corridor.

### Campbellton
- Northern New Brunswick on the Restigouche River; Hwy 11/17 junction near the Quebec border.

### Miramichi
- Eastern New Brunswick; forestry and fishing; Hwy 8/11.

### Woodstock
- Western New Brunswick on Hwy 2; agriculture; near the Houlton, Maine crossing.

### Grand Falls
- Northwestern New Brunswick on Hwy 2; potato country.

### Sussex
- Southern New Brunswick on Hwy 1; agriculture and potash.

## Newfoundland and Labrador

### St. John's — big market
- Provincial capital and easternmost market.
- No refinery on the island; all fuel arrives by sea, so it runs among the highest prices in Canada.

### Corner Brook
- Western Newfoundland; pulp and paper; western terminus of Hwy 1.

### Gander
- Central Newfoundland on Hwy 1; aviation history (Gander International).

### Labrador City
- Western Labrador iron-ore mining (IOC/Wabush); isolated, fuel trucked long distances.

## Nova Scotia

### Halifax — big market
- Largest Atlantic city; major container and auto port.
- No local refinery since the Dartmouth plant closed; fuel is imported, keeping prices high.

### Truro
- Central Nova Scotia; Hwy 102/104 junction, "the hub of Nova Scotia."

### Kentville
- Annapolis Valley agriculture on the Hwy 101 corridor.

### New Glasgow
- Northern Nova Scotia on the Trans-Canada (Hwy 104); former steel town.

### Sydney
- Cape Breton regional centre; coal and steel heritage.

### Yarmouth
- Southwestern Nova Scotia; fishing and the ferry to Maine.

## Ontario

### Toronto — big market
- Canada's largest city and freight market.
- Hwy 401 (the busiest highway in North America) and the 400-series network.
- No refinery in the city; diesel is trucked or piped from Sarnia, Nanticoke, and Montreal, so it runs above the refining centres.

### Hamilton — big market
- Canada's steel heart (ArcelorMittal Dofasco, Stelco) and a major Great Lakes port.
- QEW / Hwy 403 / LINC junction.

### Sarnia — big market
- "Chemical Valley": Imperial Oil, Suncor, and Shell refineries cluster here.
- Among the cheapest diesel in Ontario; Hwy 402 to the Port Huron, Michigan crossing.

### Ottawa — big market
- National capital on the Hwy 417/416 network; government and tech.
- Close to the Quebec border, so pricing competes with Gatineau.

### Thunder Bay — big market
- Lake Superior port and grain terminal; western terminus of the Great Lakes–St. Lawrence Seaway.
- Hwy 11/17 junction.

### Windsor
- Hwy 401 western terminus; the Ambassador Bridge, the busiest Canada–US crossing.
- Auto manufacturing centre.

### London
- Southwestern Ontario hub at the Hwy 401/402 junction; manufacturing and insurance.

### Kitchener
- Waterloo Region; Hwy 401/8; technology and advanced manufacturing.

### Oshawa
- Hwy 401 corridor; auto assembly (General Motors).

### Sudbury
- Northern Ontario mining centre (nickel, copper); Hwy 17/69 junction.

### Sault Ste. Marie
- Hwy 17 (Trans-Canada); steel (Algoma); International Bridge to Sault, Michigan.

### North Bay
- Gateway to northern Ontario; Hwy 11/17 junction; mining and forestry services.

### Timmins
- Northern Ontario gold-mining town on the Hwy 101 corridor.

### Barrie
- Hwy 400 corridor north of Toronto; warehousing and distribution growth.

### Brantford
- Hwy 403 corridor; manufacturing centre.

### Guelph
- Hwy 401/6; manufacturing and agri-food.

### Kingston
- Hwy 401; Great Lakes–St. Lawrence; institutions and logistics.

### Peterborough
- Hwy 115/7; manufacturing and agriculture.

### St. Catharines
- Niagara region on the QEW; Hwy 406.

## Prince Edward Island

### Charlottetown
- Island market; all fuel arrives by the Confederation Bridge or ferry, keeping prices among the highest in Canada.

## Quebec

### Montreal — big market
- Canada's second-largest market and a major St. Lawrence port.
- Refining cluster in Montreal East keeps prices among the cheapest in Canada.
- Hwy 20/40/15/13 network.

### Quebec City — big market
- Provincial capital and St. Lawrence port.
- Refining across the river at Lévis; Hwy 20/40.

### Gatineau
- Across the river from Ottawa on Hwy 5/50; government and tech; pricing competes with Ontario.

### Trois-Rivières
- Hwy 40/55; pulp and paper and aluminum; St. Lawrence port.

### Sherbrooke
- Eastern Townships on Hwy 10/55; manufacturing.

### Drummondville
- Hwy 20/55 junction midway between Montreal and Quebec; manufacturing and agri-food.

### Saguenay (Chicoutimi)
- Regional hub for Saguenay–Lac-Saint-Jean; aluminum (Rio Tinto); Hwy 175.

### Rimouski
- Hwy 20/132 on the St. Lawrence; maritime services.

### Val-d'Or
- Abitibi gold-mining centre on the Hwy 117 corridor; remote and northern.

### Gaspé
- Eastern Quebec on Hwy 132; fishing and forestry; remote, so prices run high.

## Saskatchewan

### Saskatoon — big market
- Largest Saskatchewan city on the Hwy 16 (Yellowhead) and Hwy 11 corridors.
- Potash and agriculture hub.

### Regina — big market
- Provincial capital at the Hwy 1 (Trans-Canada) and Hwy 6 junction.
- Agriculture and Bakken oil; home to a major refinery complex.

### Moose Jaw
- Hwy 1 (Trans-Canada); agriculture and potash; near Regina.

### Prince Albert
- Northern Saskatchewan gateway; Hwy 2/3/11 junction; forestry.
