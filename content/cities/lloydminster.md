Lloydminster straddles the border between Alberta and Saskatchewan and works as a heavy-oil service town on the Hwy 16 Yellowhead corridor. Its diesel market runs on that split. Heavy-oil service work drives demand through the region, and the Hwy 16 Yellowhead corridor moves that traffic across the provincial line and back. Pump prices in a border town like this track the cost of keeping heavy-oil service supplied along the corridor. Lloydminster prices with the Yellowhead traffic and the heavy-oil service work that runs through it.
