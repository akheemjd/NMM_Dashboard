Corner Brook sits in western Newfoundland, where the Trans-Canada Highway reaches its western terminus. Pulp and paper anchor the local economy, the industry that built the town and still defines it. Highway 1 ends here, and beyond it the island road network turns local. For a carrier this is the last full stop on the island's main corridor, a working town shaped by a single trade at the western end of Newfoundland. It marks where the national highway gives out, and that geography defines the market.
