Chicoutimi is the regional hub for Saguenay Lac Saint Jean. Aluminum anchors the local economy through Rio Tinto. Highway 175 is the main corridor that ties the city to Quebec City and the south. That single highway carries the freight that moves in and out of the whole Saguenay region. Carriers serving the area route through this hub because the loads and the supply points gather here. In the Quebec freight map, Chicoutimi stands as the northern anchor of its region, one major highway and one dominant industry holding the market together.
