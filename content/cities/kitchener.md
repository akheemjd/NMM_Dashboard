Kitchener sits in Waterloo Region on the Highway 401 and Highway 8 corridors, and its economy runs on technology and advanced manufacturing. Tech firms and advanced plants both ship freight, keeping trucks on the move through the region. That blend of high-skill industry is what drives the local fuel market. Kitchener's position on the 401 ties it into the main southern Ontario freight spine, while the Highway 8 connection links it deeper into the region's own network. That reach across both corridors is what keeps the city connected.
