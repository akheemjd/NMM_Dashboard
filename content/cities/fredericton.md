Fredericton is New Brunswick's provincial capital on the Hwy 2 corridor. Government and technology drive its economy. Sitting on Hwy 2 puts the capital on the main line across the province, tying it to the rest of New Brunswick. Government offices and technology firms fill the city, and that mix shapes the freight that moves in and out. A provincial capital on the main corridor takes its prices from its role as the seat of government and the industries that come with it.
