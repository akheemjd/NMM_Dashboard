Edmundston sits in northwestern New Brunswick on Hwy 2 at the Quebec border. Pulp and paper anchor its economy. The town is the last stop on the Hwy 2 corridor before Quebec, which makes it a border market as much as a provincial one. The mill keeps trucks loaded, and the highway runs straight through. Location at the border and a resource industry are the facts that explain this market. Both shape the freight that moves in and out of the town every day.
