Saskatoon is the largest city in Saskatchewan, sitting on the Highway 16 Yellowhead and Highway 11 corridors. Potash and agriculture anchor the local economy. The city is the biggest market in the province, the place where those two corridors come together. For a carrier this is the province's central draw, a large city built on potash and farm country at a crossing of two major highways. That combination of size, industry, and road network is what shapes how this market runs.
