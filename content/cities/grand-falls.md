Grand Falls sits in northwestern New Brunswick on Hwy 2, in potato country. Agriculture drives the local economy. The town sits on the province's main corridor across New Brunswick, surrounded by farm country, and Hwy 2 keeps it tied to the rest of the province. The potatoes keep the trucks running through, season after season. It is potato country, and the crop is the whole story. A farm town on a major corridor prices off its place on that highway and the crop that defines the region.
