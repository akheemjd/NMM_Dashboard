Red Deer sits at the midpoint of the Calgary-Edmonton run on Hwy 2 and works as an oil-patch service centre for central Alberta. Its diesel market runs on that position. Traffic moving between Calgary and Edmonton passes through the city, and the surrounding oil patch leans on Red Deer for service and supply. Pump prices in the city track the cost of fuel moving along the Hwy 2 corridor and the pace of oil-patch service work around central Alberta. Red Deer's pricing tracks the traffic and the oil-patch service work that pass through its midpoint.
