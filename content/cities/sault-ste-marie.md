Sault Ste. Marie sits on Highway 17, the Trans-Canada, and runs on steel, with Algoma anchoring the industrial base. The city also holds the International Bridge to Sault, Michigan, which carries freight across the border. Steel keeps trucks hauling raw material and finished product, and the Trans-Canada position ties the city to the movement of goods across the region. That combination of steel, a Trans-Canada corridor, and a border bridge is what shapes the local fuel market, anchoring it to both the steel trade and the freight crossing at the border.
