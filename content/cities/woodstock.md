Woodstock sits in western New Brunswick on Hwy 2, in farm country near the Houlton, Maine crossing. Agriculture drives the local economy. Being on Hwy 2 and close to a US crossing puts the town on a trade line as much as a highway. Trucks heading to and from Maine pass through, and the farms keep local freight moving. The town sits close to the border, and the farms sit all around it. Location near the border and an agricultural base are the facts that explain this market.
