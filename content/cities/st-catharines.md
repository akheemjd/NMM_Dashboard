St. Catharines sits in the Niagara region on the QEW, with Highway 406 running through the city. The QEW is the main freight corridor through the region, tying St. Catharines to the movement of goods across the Niagara peninsula, and Highway 406 connects the city to the surrounding area. That position on the QEW and Highway 406 is what shapes the local fuel market, anchoring the city to the flow of traffic that moves through the Niagara region day in and day out.
