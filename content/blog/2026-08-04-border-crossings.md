Peace Bridge is running a 9 minute delay. Queenston-Lewiston has 5 minutes. The numbers are close. The crossing experience is not.

Peace Bridge has FAST lanes. Commercial traffic moves through a dedicated priority lane. Queenston-Lewiston does not have FAST. Every truck sits in the same queue as passenger cars.

Five minutes sounds trivial. But the FAST lane at Peace Bridge means that 9 minute number applies to general traffic. A truck with FAST clearance skips the line entirely. At Queenston-Lewiston, the 5 minute delay applies to everyone. No priority lane exists. On a busy Friday afternoon, that gap widens fast.

Two other crossings lack FAST lanes. Thousand Islands Bridge at Lansdowne is running clear today. No delay. But commercial shares the same lane as every minivan crossing for vacation. That becomes a problem when the line builds. There is no way around it.

Pacific Highway in Surrey is the other crossing with a delay. 5 minutes at Blaine. FAST lanes are available. Nexus too. The delay is modest and FAST clears it. If you are hauling into the Pacific Northwest, this is your crossing. Zero reason to look elsewhere.

The Ontario border looks clean otherwise. Ambassador Bridge is flowing. No delay at Windsor-Detroit. Blue Water Bridge in Sarnia is clear. Ambassador moves over 8,000 trucks a day and is holding zero wait. Lacolle into New York is open with no delay and FAST running. Coutts-Sweetgrass in Alberta is clear. Emerson-Pembina in Manitoba is clear.

Nine crossings tracked live on the dashboard. Only two lack FAST lanes. Both sit on the Ontario-New York corridor. Queenston-Lewiston and Thousand Islands Bridge. Everything else offers a priority lane for pre-cleared commercial traffic.

If you are crossing at Niagara and you have FAST clearance, Peace Bridge is the answer. Nine minutes on the general line does not touch you. If you do not have FAST, Queenston-Lewiston at 5 minutes looks better on paper. Until it does not. That line is one accident or one inspection blitz away from a backup with no priority exit.

Coming from the GTA, Peace Bridge is your first hit on the QEW. Another 10 minutes down the road gets you to Queenston-Lewiston. The extra drive is worth it when Peace spikes above 30 minutes. But every truck sitting in that Queenston line shares the lane with a Honda Civic. When the backup hits, there is no bypass.

The best crossing is the one with a FAST lane. Every time.
