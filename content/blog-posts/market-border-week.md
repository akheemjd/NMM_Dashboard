Peace Bridge ran 13 minutes this week. That is the longest commercial wait at any crossing the Canada Border Services Agency reported, and it is the one number below that would move a routing decision.

Canada US border wait times sat quiet everywhere else. Ambassador Bridge, Blue Water Bridge, Lacolle, Coutts-Sweetgrass and Emerson-Pembina all reported clear. Queenston-Lewiston ran 7 minutes. Pacific Highway ran 5.

Thirteen minutes will not sink a load. It is a rounding error against a bad Tuesday at a congested port. It matters at the margin all the same. It is the difference between making a delivery window and calling ahead to move it, and it is the reason to check the [Peace Bridge board](https://dashboard.northernmilemedia.com/border-wait-times/peace-bridge/) before a pickup time gets set rather than after.

The rest of the border is the real story. Five clear crossings and three small delays is a good week, and good weeks are exactly when schedules get built on the assumption that nothing will move. The [border trends page](https://dashboard.northernmilemedia.com/border-trends/) keeps those readings over time if you want to compare a quiet week against the ones that were not.

## Diesel held at 268.5 cents with an 80.4 cent spread

## The loonie at 1.4145

The Bank of Canada had CAD/USD at 1.4145 on September 25, with the loonie weakening 0.06 percent. Diesel is priced in US dollars on the wholesale market and bought with Canadian dollars. A weaker loonie raises the Canadian cost of the same barrel even when the barrel itself has not moved.

{{figure:fx-trend}}

A 0.06 percent move is noise on its own, and it should be treated as noise. The level is the part that compounds across a year of fuel spend. A rate near 1.41 means every US dollar purchase takes more out of the account than it did at a stronger loonie.

The US Energy Information Administration has US diesel at $6.529 a gallon in its latest reading. That is the figure a counterpart south of the line is working from when a rate gets discussed, and it is why a cross-border lane never lines up neatly against a Canadian cost sheet.

## What to do with this

Fuel where the spread favours you. The 80.4 cent gap between 294.3 in Quebec and 213.9 in the Northwest Territories is the widest lever on the board this week, and it is a routing question rather than a negotiation.

Check the border board before you commit to a pickup time. Thirteen minutes on the Peace Bridge is manageable. The point is that you knew before you left the yard.

Watch the level, not the tick. A 0.06 percent currency move is not a signal.

## Where this leaves the week

Canada US border wait times were the quiet part of this week and the diesel spread was the loud part. Neither number is urgent. Both are published, which means neither is a surprise you have to absorb after the fact.
