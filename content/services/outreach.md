# Outreach: data hub engagements

Replaces the backlink ask. The old emails asked associations for a link, which
costs them effort and earns you almost nothing. This asks for a conversation
about something they already want and cannot staff.

Rules
- Send by hand, one at a time. Never bulk, never automated. A scripted blast
  gets you filtered and burns the domain.
- Short. If it does not fit on a phone screen, cut it.
- One follow-up. Then stop.
- Never claim a number you cannot show them live.

## What not to do

- Do not send the media kit. It is priced for newsletter sponsorship and will
  confuse the ask.
- Do not lead with the price. Lead with the live thing they can look at.
- Do not send to `info@` and expect a reply. Find a named person. Association
  sites usually list staff and directors.
- Do not follow up more than once.
