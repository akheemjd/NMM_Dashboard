# Tuesday Carousel Template — Border Crossings

Build a 5-slide LinkedIn carousel PDF for Northern Mile Media. Export as 1080×1080 px square slides, PDF format. Dark theme.

## Design System
Background: #0B0D11 (deep blue-black)
Primary text: #F2F4F7 (near-white)
Secondary text: #A8B2BE (gray)
Accent: #1E9E66 (green)
Yellow: #F5C518
Red: #E5484D
Font: system sans-serif (no web fonts needed)

## Brand
Northern Mile Media — Canadian trucking dashboard and newsletter. Voice: dispatcher-analyst. Data-first, no fluff.

## Data (pull live from dashboard before building)
Fetch: https://dashboard.northernmilemedia.com/data/border.json
→ crossings array: name, wait, delay_minutes, status

## Slide Content

### Slide 1 — Cover
Logo: "NORTHERN MILE" top left, green. Slide number: "1/5" top right, gray.
Headline: "Before you cross" (or specific observation from today's data)
Subtitle: One sentence on today's border conditions
Footer: Today's date

### Slide 2 — The Crossing That Matters Today
Big name centered: the crossing with the most notable condition today (busiest, or clearest)
Below: wait time in large green text
Subtitle: What this means for routing decisions
Footer: "CBSA · updated every 30 min"

### Slide 3 — All Crossings Dashboard
Show a clean visual of 3-5 major crossings with wait times
Each: crossing name | wait time | colored dot (green=flowing, yellow=moderate, red=heavy)
Headline: "Commercial wait times right now"

### Slide 4 — The Strategy
Headline: "What a dispatcher does with this"
Relatable scenario: "Ambassador is backed up. Blue Water is flowing. That's a 20-minute detour that saves 45 minutes."
Or: "All crossings clear. Days like this you take the fastest route and bank the time."
Footer: "Source: Northern Mile dashboard — live data"

### Slide 5 — CTA
Headline: "We check all 9 crossings every 30 minutes so you don't have to."
URL in green: "northernmilemedia.com"
Subtitle: "Get the Wednesday Snapshot"
Footer: "dashboard.northernmilemedia.com — free Canadian trucking data"

## Rules
No emojis, no AI vocabulary, no fluff. Clean typography, strong hierarchy. Dark theme. 5 pages, 1080×1080 each.
