# Directory & Listing Submissions

Paste-and-submit list for getting Northern Mile cited in legitimate directories.
Low ceiling per item, but together they build the citation footprint Google wants
before it ranks a young domain — and two of these (Product Hunt, AlternativeTo)
can drive real traffic on their own.

**Core positioning — reuse this wording everywhere:**
- One-liner: *"Free cross-border trucking data — Canadian + US diesel, border waits, USD/CAD, and your rate floor. Every number dated and sourced."*
- Longer: *"Northern Mile is a free dashboard for cross-border trucking. It publishes the North American Diesel Index (NRCan + EIA, equal weight), live CBSA border wait times, the Bank of Canada exchange rate, road closures, and a rate-floor calculator — no paywall, no login, every figure dated and linked to its source."*

**Canonical URLs:**
- Dashboard: `https://dashboard.northernmilemedia.com`
- Barometer (the citable page): `https://dashboard.northernmilemedia.com/freight-barometer/`
