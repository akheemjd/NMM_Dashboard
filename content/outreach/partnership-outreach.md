Templates for co-promotion with trucking associations, carriers, and adjacent media. Short, direct, and low-ask. Send from northernmilemedia@gmail.com.

## Template 1 — Association newsletter cross-promotion

**Subject:** A free weekly fuel + border snapshot for [association] members

Hi [name],

Northern Mile Media publishes a free dashboard for Canadian trucking — diesel prices across 69 cities, cross-border wait times, USD/CAD, and a rate-floor calculator — from primary sources (NRCan, Bank of Canada, CBSA, EIA).

I'd like to put it in front of [association] members. Two ways, both free for you:

1. A short mention in your next member email — we can draft the two lines.
2. A recurring "fuel snapshot" — a 3–4 line weekly fuel/border update you can drop into your newsletter, credited to Northern Mile Media.

There's no cost and no commitment. Your members get a useful tool, and we get discovered by the people who'd actually use it.

Worth a look? https://dashboard.northernmilemedia.com/
