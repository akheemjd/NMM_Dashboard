# Cross-Border Cost Brief — cold outreach sequence

Four emails. No calls. Everything a carrier needs to decide is in the message.

**Rules that make this not spam:**
- Send to a **publicly published business address**, about **their** business. That specific case is
  permitted under CASL without prior consent.
- **Real sender name and postal address in every message.** No "The Team".
- **One unsubscribe line, honoured immediately.** No dark patterns.
- **Never more than 4 emails.** After the fourth, stop. Forever.
- **Do not send to a role address** (`info@`, `sales@`) if a named one exists.

## After the fourth email

**Stop.** No re-engagement campaign, no "just checking in" in six months. A list you email five times
is a list that reports you.

## What to do with a "sample" reply

Send the generated HTML from `output/reports/` as a PDF attachment, in a plain email, with one line:
*"Here is last month's. Happy to answer anything."* No pitch in the reply. The document is the pitch.

## What to do with a "stop"

Remove immediately, confirm removal in one line, never contact again. Log it.

## Tracking that matters

Only three numbers: **replies**, **samples sent**, **subscriptions started**. Open rates are not
reliable and will only make you doubt a working message.
