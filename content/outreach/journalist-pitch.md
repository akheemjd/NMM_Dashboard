# Journalist Pitch — Northern Mile (US trade media)

## Why this framing works

Cold "please link to me" gets ~1–3% response. This pitch leads with a **number or
asset the writer can actually use**, and only names the site as the source of that
thing. One specific, dated, citable fact beats a link request every time.

**The citable assets:**
- **NADI** — the North American Diesel Index (NRCan + EIA, equal weight). Nobody else publishes it.
- **Cross-border floor calculator** — Toronto → Chicago with US PADD fuel, real road distances, and a rate floor. Free, no login.
- **Barometer** — one dated screen with a "cite this" block. `dashboard.northernmilemedia.com/freight-barometer/`

Always link to the **barometer** — it's the stable, dated, citable page.
